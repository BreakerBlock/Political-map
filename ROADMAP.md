- **2026-11-05 — Fix: photos failing on desktop (Windows/Mac).** **Cause:** the site rewrote every Wikipedia thumbnail URL to an arbitrary width — `/1600px-` for story slides, the stories teaser and the Surprise-me card on desktop (`/960px-` on phones), `/500px-` in popups. Wikimedia now only serves a fixed set of thumbnail widths (…250, 330, 500, 960, 1280, 1920…) and refuses non-standard sizes and upscales beyond the original file, so on desktop the 1600px story/Surprise photos failed outright, and popup photos of originals narrower than 500px failed everywhere; phones mostly worked because 960 happens to be a standard width. A failed summary fetch (e.g. a 429 when several stories pre-load at once) was also cached as "no photo" for the whole session, and the teaser's CSS background had no fallback at all. **Fix (index.html):** one shared photo helper — summaries go through a small queue (max 4 at once), retry once on HTTP 429 (honouring `Retry-After`, ≤4 s), and transient failures are retried after a minute instead of being cached; `wikiImgSet()` returns an ordered list of candidates: a **standard width never wider than the original** (popups 330/500 by screen density; stories/Surprise 960, 1280 or 1920 by screen size), then the unmodified `thumbnail.source`, then `originalimage` (web formats only, ≤6000px; used first when the original is already smaller than needed). Every `<img>` walks that list on `error` (popups via `data-alt` + `imgFail()`, stories/Surprise via `imgChain()`), and the teaser only uses a URL that actually loaded; images get `decoding="async"` and an explicit `referrerpolicy`. **Tested** in headless Chromium against a mock that behaves like Wikimedia (rejects non-standard widths with 429 and upscales with 400, 429s a quarter of summaries once): before the fix 1 of 5 story photos rendered at 1366px; after it, 6/6 popups, 5/5 story slides and 3/3 Surprise cards rendered at both 1366px and 390px with no requests rejected and no JS errors. (WebKit/Firefox aren't installed in the sandbox, so those were reasoned from the code: nothing browser-specific is used.)
